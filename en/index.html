CBCA Method

Structures change. Countries change. Laws change. A well-designed architecture remains.

That is why we apply Cross Border Capital Architecture (CBCA). You are not hiring separate paperwork: you are applying a method that always answers the same six questions. Scroll to review them one by one.

The CBCA Methodology unfolds across four modules.

01
Everything starts here

Horizon Europe Architecture™

The complete legal map of your group in Europe, drawn up before the first deed is signed. We define real ownership and control, dividend flow and the role of each entity; we incorporate the investment vehicles (SPVs) and the companies in the right jurisdiction. You don't handle paperwork: you receive a complete, operating architecture in the Netherlands, Luxembourg, Spain or France.

02

Multi-Jurisdictional Wealth Preservation Model™

Your wealth protected to endure, not just to pay less tax this year. We place each asset in the right entity and jurisdiction, planning generational transfer from today, and apply the treaties that prevent double taxation across Mexico, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, France and Spain. A well-designed architecture doesn't just protect: it keeps capital active and productive. The end goal is not to preserve capital, but to let it keep creating wealth.

03

International Operational Activation™

The step that turns a legal structure into a group that truly operates. We open corporate accounts at European banks for newly incorporated entities with foreign shareholders. Without this, the architecture is paper; with it, your capital moves.

04

CBCA Evidence Protocol™

So your structure withstands any tax review, every year it operates. We document and maintain the evidence that your companies genuinely operate where they claim to — office, staff, real activity — exactly as required under European anti-abuse regulation.

The other three modules exist because Horizon Europe Architecture™ exists first. Together, they are the practical application of the method to your mandate.

Representative Mandates

Every mandate has its own architecture.

Three real mandates, each with the CBCA module applied. Identifying details have been omitted for confidentiality; references are verifiable under NDA.

Horizon Europe Architecture™ · Technology sector

Mexican technology group — European expansion with a 2030 horizon.

Parent company in the Netherlands to centralise intellectual property, technology and licences with access to all 27 EU member states; operating subsidiaries in Spain and the United Kingdom; treasury in Luxembourg.

Resulting architecture: the group now operates from the Netherlands, with active subsidiaries in Spain and the United Kingdom and treasury ready to receive private capital.

Multi-Jurisdictional Wealth Preservation Model™ · Real estate investment

LATAM dividends without double taxation for real-estate investment in Spain.

Spanish company under the E.T.V.E. regime — exempting dividends and capital gains sourced outside Spain — so non-resident partners receive distributions free of Spanish withholding tax. The capital is deployed into real estate in Spain.

Resulting architecture: they capture Spanish rental income without the friction of double taxation.

Horizon Europe Architecture™ · Growth capital

KlinkApp — access to European institutional capital for Latin American investors.

Dutch company as a co-investment vehicle to pool Latin American capital in a European growth round, with a clear co-investment agreement, a clean cap table and economic substance documented from incorporation. Banking included.

Resulting architecture: the capital enters the round through a structure that the institutional investor recognises and approves without friction.
